**SOLARA BAY: island-city crime game — 12 story missions, 5-star police and helicopter chases, crew and respect ranks, garage upgrades, car radio, safehouse saves.**

Mara Vale owes the harbor crew. One week, twelve jobs, one island. Steal cars, outrun 5-star police with a helicopter overhead, recruit Dante Reyes to your crew, climb the respect ranks from Drifter to Queen of Solara, and finish at the lighthouse. Free roam includes taxi fares, vigilante bounties, 10 sunken caches, a safehouse shop with garage upgrades, 4 weapons with a weapon wheel, NOS boosts, and 3 synthesized radio stations.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://127.0.0.1:8080
```

WASD move / drive · Mouse orbit camera · E steal / exit car · Space brake · Shift run / NOS · 1–4 weapons · Tab weapon wheel · R reload · Q radio · F shop at garage · M start mission · Esc pause.
